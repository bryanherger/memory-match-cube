How to play

  • Tap a square to reveal its tile.
  • Tap a second square to look for its match — anywhere on the cube.
  • Drag to rotate the cube; matches can be on another face, edge, or corner.
  • Edges and corners show the same tile on every side they face.
  • Clear every tile to solve the cube. Fewer distinct tiles means an easier game.
